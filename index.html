Welcome to Nepal

Nepal is a landlocked country in South Asia, located between China and India. Its capital is Kathmandu. Nepal is known for its diverse landscapes, cultures, and the Himalayas, including Mount Everest.

Location

Nepal lies in South Asia, with mountain, hill, and Terai regions.

History

Nepal has a rich history of kingdoms, traditions, architecture, and cultural heritage.

World geography

Nepal is home to Mount Everest, the world's highest mountain above sea level, and many other Himalayan peaks.

Design Rationale

  1. Primary user and task: The primary users are tourists interested in visiting Nepal. The interface helps them discover destinations, read about places, and explore travel information.
  2. Visual hierarchy: The large "Discover the beauty of Nepal" heading and mountain photograph attract attention first. The main action, "Explore destinations," leads visitors to the next step.
  3. Grouping: The destination cards group each place's name, photograph, description, and details link together. This makes the information easier to scan.
  4. Appropriate control: The review page uses a select menu for choosing a destination because visitors should choose from a short list of available places. Labeled text fields and a textarea collect feedback.
  5. Discoverability and cognitive load: The same four navigation links appear at the top of every page, so visitors do not need to remember how to return to another section.
  6. Responsive design: CSS media queries change the card grid to one column on smaller screens. Navigation wraps, the hero stacks vertically, and controls expand to fit the screen.
  7. Accessibility: Photographs have descriptive alternative text, form controls have labels, headings follow a logical order, and visible keyboard focus styles help keyboard users navigate.
